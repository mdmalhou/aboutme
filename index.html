01 / Research

Learning to solve
hard problems.

My work asks a simple question: can learning systems discover strategies for problems that resist traditional algorithms?

01

Formal mathematics

Theorem proving in Lean using agent self-play and HyperTree Proof Search, with an emphasis on scalable mathematical reasoning.

02

Learning algorithms

MCTS and reinforcement learning systems that formulate hard mathematical problems as sequential decision processes.

03

AI cryptanalysis

Neural attacks on lattice-, code-, and multivariate-based cryptosystems, including schemes proposed for the post-quantum era.
